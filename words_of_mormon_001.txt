Words of Mormon Chapter 1

¶ 1:1 And now Y, Mormon, am aboute to bitake the record which Y haue be makynge in to my ſone Moronyes hondis; lo! Y haue witneſſid almeſt al the diſtruccioun of my puple, the Nephitis.
¶ 1:2 ⁊ it is many hundrid ȝeeris aftir the comyng of Criſt that Y bitake theſe recordis in to my ſones hondis; and it ſuppoſith me that he ſchal witneſſe the hool diſtruccioun of my puple / But mai God graunte that he mai ouerlyue hem, that he mai write ſumwhat of hem, and ſumwhat of Criſt, that perauenture ſum dai it mai profite hem.
¶ 1:3 ⁊ now Y ſpeke ſumwhat of that whiche Y haue writun; for aftir that Y hadde maad an abreggyng fro the platis of Nephi, doun to the regne of this kyng Beniamyn, of whom Amaleki ſpak, Y ſouȝte among the recordis whiche hadden be bitakun in to myn hondis, and Y foond theſe platis, whiche conteyneden this ſmal accompt of the prophetis, fro Jacob doun to the regne of this kyng Beniamyn, and alſo many of Nephies wordis.
¶ 1:4 ⁊ the thingis whiche ben on theſe platis pleſiden me, for the prophecies of the comyng of Criſt; and my fadris wiſten that many of hem hadden be fillid; ȝhe, and Y alſo woot that as many thingis as han be prophecied of vs doun to this dai han be fillid, and as many as paſſen ouer this dai moten ſureli come to paſſe.
¶ 1:5 Wherfor Y chees theſe thingis, to finyſche my record on hem, which remenaunt of my record Y ſchal take fro the platis of Nephi; and Y can not write the hundrid part of my puplis thingis.
¶ 1:6 But lo! Y ſchal take theſe platis, whiche conteynen theſe prophecyingis and reuelaciouns, and putte hem with the remenaunt of my record, for thei ben choſun to me; and Y woot that thei ſchulen be choſun to my britheren.
¶ 1:7 ⁊ Y do this for a wiſe cauſe; for thus it whiſprith to me, aftir the worchyngis of the Spirit of the Lord which is in me / And now, Y knowe not alle thingis; but the Lord knowith alle thingis whiche ben to come; wherfor he worchith in me to do aftir his wille.
¶ 1:8 ⁊ my preier to God is for my britheren, that thei mai ones aȝen come to the knowyng of God, ȝhe, the aȝenbiȝng of Criſt; that thei mai ones aȝen be a delitable puple.
¶ 1:9 ⁊ now Y, Mormon, procede to finyſche out my record, which Y take fro the platis of Nephi; and Y make it aftir the knowyng and the vndirſtondyng which God hath ȝouun to me.
¶ 1:10 Wherfor, whanne Amaleki hadde bitakun theſe platis in to kyng Beniamyns hondis, he took hem and puttide hem with the othere platis, that conteyneden recordis handid doun bi the kyngis, fro generacioun to generacioun, til to kyng Beniamyns daies.
¶ 1:11 ⁊ thei weren handid doun fro kyng Beniamyn, fro generacioun to generacioun, til thei ben fallen in to myn hondis / And Y, Mormon, preie to God that thei be preſerued fro this tyme forth / And Y woot wel that thei ſchulen be preſerued; for grete thingis ben writun in hem, wherbi my puple and her britheren ſchulen be demed at the greet and laſte dai, aftir Goddis word that is writun.
¶ 1:12 Now, as touchynge this kyng Beniamyn, he hadde ſumwhat of ſtryues among his owne puple.
¶ 1:13 Alſo the armees of Lamanytis camen doun out of the lond of Nephi, to bateile aȝens his puple / But lo! Kyng Beniamyn gaderide togidere hiſe armees, and ſtood aȝens hem; and he fauȝt with the ſtrengthe of his owne arm, with Labans ſwerd.
¶ 1:14 ⁊ in the ſtrengthe of the Lord thei ſtryueden aȝens her enemyes, til thei hadden ſlayn many thouſyndis of Lamanytis / And thei ſtryueden aȝens the Lamanytis til thei hadden dryuun hem out of alle the londis of her eritage.
¶ 1:15 ⁊ aftir ther hadden be falſe Criſtis, and the mouthis of hem hadden be ſtoppid, and thei weren punyſchid aftir her ſynnes;
¶ 1:16 ⁊ aftir ther hadden be falſe prophetis, and falſe precheris and techeris among the puple, and alle theſe weren punyſchid aftir her ſynnes; and aftir ther hadde be myche ſtryf and many diſcenciouns awei to the Lamanytis, lo! Kyng Beniamyn, with the help of the hooli prophetis that weren among his puple,
¶ 1:17 For lo! Kyng Beniamyn was an hooli man, and he regnede ouer his puple in riȝtfulneſſe; and ther weren many hooli men in the lond, and thei ſpaken Goddis word with power and with auctoritee; and thei vſeden myche ſharpneſſe for the ſtif-neckidneſſe of the puple;
¶ 1:18 Wherfor, with the help of theſe, kyng Beniamyn, bi trauelynge with al the myȝt of his bodi and the vertu of his hool ſoule, and alſo the prophetis, ones more eſtabliſchide pees in the lond.

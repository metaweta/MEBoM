The prophecie of Nephi, the ſone of Helaman / God manaces the puple of Nephi that he ſchal viſite hem in hiſe wraththe, to her ful diſtriȝng, ne but if thei do penaunce of her wickidneſſe. God ſmytith the puple of Nephi with peſtilence; thei don penaunce and turnen aȝen to hym. Samuel, a Lamanyte, prophecieth to the Nephitis.

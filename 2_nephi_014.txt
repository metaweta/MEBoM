2 Nephi Chapter 14

¶ 14:1 And in that dai ſeuen wymmen ſchulen take hoold of o man, and ſeien, We ſchulen ete oure owne breed, and were oure owne clothis; oneli be we clepid bi thi name, to take awei oure ſchenſchipe.
¶ 14:2 In that dai the braunche of the Lord ſchal be fair and glorious, and the fruyt of the erthe ſchal be excellent and ſemeli to hem that ben aſcapid of Iſrael.
¶ 14:3 ⁊ it ſchal be that thei that ben left in Sion, and dwellen in Jeruſalem, ſchulen be clepid hooli, ech man that is writun among the lyuynge in Jeruſalem,
¶ 14:4 Whanne the Lord ſchal haue waiſchun awei the filthe of Siouns douȝtris, and ſchal haue purgid the blood of Jeruſalem fro the myddis therof, bi the ſpirit of doom, and bi the ſpirit of brennynge.
¶ 14:5 ⁊ the Lord ſchal make on ech dwellyng place of the hil of Sion, and on hir gaderyngis, a cloude and ſmoke bi dai, and the ſchynyng of a flawmynge fier bi niȝt; for on al the glorie of Sion ſchal be a defenſe.
¶ 14:6 ⁊ ther ſchal be a tabernacle for a ſchadewe in the dai tyme fro the heete, and for a place of refuyt, and a hidyng fro tempeſt and fro reyn.

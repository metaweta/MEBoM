Moroni Chapter 1

¶ 1:1 Now Y, Moroni, aftir Y hadde maad an ende of abreggynge the accounte of Jaredis puple, hadde ſuppoſid not to haue writun more, but Y haue not as ȝit periſchid; and Y make not my ſilf knowun to the Lamanytis leſt thei ſchulden diſtrie me.
¶ 1:2 For lo! Her werris ben ful fers among hem ſilf; and for her hatrede thei putten to deeth euery Nephite that wole not denye the Criſt.
¶ 1:3 ⁊ Y, Moroni, wole not denye the Criſt; wherfor, Y wandre whiderſoeuere Y may for the ſaufte of myn owne lijf.
¶ 1:4 Wherfor, Y write a fewe mo thingis, contrarie to that whiche Y hadde ſuppoſid; for Y hadde ſuppoſid not to haue writun ony more; but Y write a fewe mo thingis, that perauenture thei mowe be of worth to my britheren, the Lamanytis, in ſum future dai, aftir the wille of the Lord.

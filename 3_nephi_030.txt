3 Nephi Chapter 30

¶ 30:1 Herkne, O ye hethene men, and here the wordis of Jheſu Criſt, the Sone of the lyuynge God, whiche he hath comaundid me for to ſpeke of you; for lo! He comaundith me for to write, and ſeie,
¶ 30:2 Turne ye, alle ye hethene men, fro youre wickid weies; and do ye penaunce of youre ȝuel doyngis, of youre leſyngis and diſceyuyngis, of youre fornycaciouns, of youre priuei abhomynaciouns, of youre ydolatries, of youre manſleyingis, of youre preeſthedis, of youre enuyes, of youre ſtryues, and fro al youre wickidneſſe and abhomynaciouns; and come ye to me, and be ye baptiſid in my name, that ye moun reſſeyue remyſſioun of youre ſynnes, and be fillid with the Hooli Gooſt, that ye moun be noumbrid with my puple that ben of Iſraels hous.

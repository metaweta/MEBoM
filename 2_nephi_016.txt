2 Nephi Chapter 16

¶ 16:1 In the ȝeer that kyng Ozie diede, Y ſiȝ alſo the Lord ſittynge on a trone, hiȝ and lift vp, and hiſe ſkirtis fillide the temple.
¶ 16:2 Aboue it ſtoden ſerafyns; ech oon hadde ſixe wengis; with tweyne he hilide his face, and with tweyne he hilide hiſe feet, and with tweyne he dide fle.
¶ 16:3 ⁊ oon cryede to another, and ſeide, Hooli, hooli, hooli is the Lord of ooſtis; al the erthe is ful of his glorie.
¶ 16:4 ⁊ the poſtis of the dore weren moued at the vois of hym that cryede, and the hous was fillid with ſmoke.
¶ 16:5 Thanne Y ſeide, Wo is to me! For Y am vndon; for Y am a man of vncleene lippis, and dwelle in the myddis of a puple of vncleene lippis; for myn iȝen han ſeyn the kyng, the Lord of ooſtis.
¶ 16:6 ⁊ oon of the ſeraphyns fley to me, hauynge a quyk cool in his hond, which he hadde takun with the tongis fro the auter;
¶ 16:7 ⁊ he leide it on my mouth, and ſeide, Lo, this hath touchid thi lippis; and thi wickidneſſe is takun awei, and thi ſynne purgid.
¶ 16:8 Alſo Y herde the vois of the Lord, ſeiynge, Whom ſchal Y ſende, and who ſchal go for vs? Thanne Y ſeide, Lo, Y am here; ſende thou me.
¶ 16:9 ⁊ he ſeide, Go thou, and ſeie to this puple, Here ye in deede, but ye vndurſtoden not; and ſe ye in deede, but ye perceyueden not.
¶ 16:10 Make the herte of this puple fat, and make hiſe eeris heuy, and cloſe hiſe iȝen, leſt he ſe with hiſe iȝen, and here with hiſe eeris, and vndurſtonde with his herte, and be conuertid, and Y hele hym.
¶ 16:11 Thanne Y ſeide, Lord, hou longe? And he ſeide, Til the citees be maad deſolat with out dwellere, and the houſis with out man, and the lond be lefte forſakun.
¶ 16:12 ⁊ the Lord ſchal do men fer awei, for ther ſchal be a greet forſakynge in the myddis of the lond.
¶ 16:13 But ȝit ther ſchal be therynne a tenthing, and it ſchal be conuertid, and it ſchal be in to ſchewing, as a terebynte is, and as an ook, that ſpredith forth hiſe boowis; that ſchal be hooli ſeed, that ſchal ſtonde therynne.

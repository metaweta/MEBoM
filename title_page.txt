The book of Mormon: an account writun bi the hond of Mormon on plates takun fro the plates of Nephi. Wherfor, it is an abreggyng of the record of the puple of Nephi, and alſo of the Lamanytis / writun to the Lamanytis, whiche ben a remenaunt of the hous of Iſrael, and alſo to Jew and to Gentil / writun bi weie of comaundement, and alſo bi the ſpirit of prophecie and of reuelacioun / writun and ſelid vp, and hidde vp to the Lord, that thei ſchulden not be diſtried / to come forth bi the ȝifte and the power of God to the interpretynge therof / ⁊ ſelid bi the hond of Moroni, and hidde vp to the Lord, to come forth in due tyme bi weie of the Gentil / the interpretynge therof bi the ȝifte of God.

An abreggyng takun fro the book of Ether alſo, whiche is a record of the puple of Jared, that weren ſcaterid at the tyme whanne the Lord confoundide the langage of the puple, whanne thei bilden a tour to gete to heuene / whiche is to ſchewe to the remenaunt of the hous of Iſrael what grete thingis the Lord hath do for her fadris, and that thei may knowe the couenauntis of the Lord, that thei ben not caſt out for euere / ⁊ alſo to the conuyncynge of the Jew and the Gentil that Jheſu is the Criſt, the euerlaſtynge God, ſchewynge hym ſilf to alle folkis / ⁊ now, if ther ben defautes, thei ben the errouris of men; wherfor, condempne not the thingis of God, that ye may be founden ſpotles at the domes ſete of Criſt.

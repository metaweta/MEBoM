Moroni Chapter 2

¶ 2:1 The wordis of Criſt, that he ſpak to hiſe diſciplis, the twelue whom he hadde choſen, as he leide hiſe hondis vpon hem—
¶ 2:2 ⁊ he clepide hem bi name, and ſeide, Ye ſchulen clepe to the fadir in my name, in ſtrong preier; and whanne ye han do this, ye ſchulen haue power, that to hym on whom ye leyen youre hondis, ye ſchulen ȝyue the Hooli Gooſt; and in my name ſchulen ye it ȝyue, for ſo doon myn apoſtlis.
¶ 2:3 Now Criſt ſpak theſe wordis to hem in the tyme of his firſte comyng; and the puple herde it not, but hiſe diſciplis herden / And on as many as thei leiden her hondis, felde the Hooli Gooſt.

Mosiah Chapter 6

¶ 6:1 And now, kyng Beniamyn thouȝte it was expedient, aftir he hadde endid ſpekynge to the puple, that he ſchulde take the names of alle tho that hadden entrid in to a couenaunt with God to kepe hiſe comaundementis.
¶ 6:2 ⁊ ther was not oon ſoule, no but it were litle children, but that hadde entrid in to the couenaunt and hadde takun vpon hem the name of Criſt.
¶ 6:3 ⁊ alſo, whanne kyng Beniamyn hadde maad an ende of alle theſe thingis, and hadde ſacrid his ſone Moſie to be a gouernour and a kyng ouer his puple, and hadde ȝouun hym alle the chargis of the rewme, and alſo hadde ordeyned preeſtis to teche the puple, that therbi thei myȝten here and knowe the comaundementis of God, and to ſtire hem vp in mynde of the ooth whiche thei hadden maad, he lefte the multitude, and thei turneden aȝen, euery oon, aftir her meynees, to her owne houſis.
¶ 6:4 ⁊ Moſie bigan to regne in his fadris ſtede / And he bigan to regne in the thrittithe ȝeer of his age, makynge in the hole, aboute foure hundrid and ſixe and ſeuenti ȝeer fro the tyme that Lethi lefte Jeruſalem.
¶ 6:5 ⁊ kyng Beniamyn liuede thre ȝeer, and he diede.
¶ 6:6 ⁊ kyng Moſie walkide in the weies of the Lord, and kepte hiſe doomes and hiſe ſtatutis, and kepte hiſe comaundementis in alle thingis what euere he comaundide hym.
¶ 6:7 ⁊ kyng Moſie made his puple that thei ſchulden tilie the erthe / And he alſo, hym ſilf, tiliede the erthe, that therbi he myȝte not be maad greuous to his puple, that he myȝte do aftir that his fadir hadde doon in alle thingis / And ther was no ſtrijf among al his puple bi the ſpace of thre ȝeer.

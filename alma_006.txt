Alma Chapter 6

¶ 6:1 And whanne Alma hadde maad an ende of ſpekynge to the puple of the chirche, that was eſtabliſid in the citee of Zerahemla, he ordeynede preeſtis and eldris, bi the leiȝnge on of hiſe hondis, aftir the ordre of God, to preſide and to kepe the chirche.
¶ 6:2 ⁊ who ſo euer was not of the chirche that dide penaunce of her ſynnes weren baptiſid in to penaunce, and weren takun in to the chirche.
¶ 6:3 ⁊ alſo who ſo euer was of the chirche that dide not penaunce of her wickidneſſe and mekede not hem ſilf bifor God, Y meene hem that weren reiſid in the pride of her hertis, the ſame weren caſt out, and her names weren doon awei, that her names weren not noumbrid among the names of the riȝtful.
¶ 6:4 ⁊ thus thei bigunnen to ordeyne the ordre of the chirche in the citee of Zerahemla.
¶ 6:5 Now Y wolde that ye ſchulden vndirſtonde, that the word of God was liberal to alle, that noon weren depryued of the priuylegie of gaderynge hem ſilf togidere to here the word of God.
¶ 6:6 Netheles the ſones of God weren comaundid, that thei ſchulden gadere hem ſilf togidere ofte, and ioyne in faſtynge and myȝti preier for the wele of the ſoulis of hem that knewen not God.
¶ 6:7 ⁊ whanne Alma hadde maad theſe ordenaunces, he departide fro hem, ȝhe, fro the chirche that was in the citee of Zerahemla, and ȝede ouer on the eeſt of the flood Sidon, in to the valei of Gedeon, wher a citee was bildid, which was clepid the citee of Gedeon, which was in the valei that was clepid Gedeon, clepid aftir the man that Nehor ſlouy with the ſwerd.
¶ 6:8 ⁊ Alma ȝede and bigan to declare the word of God to the chirche that was eſtabliſid in the valei of Gedeon, aftir the ſchewyng of the treuthe of the word which hiſe fadris hadden ſpokun, and aftir the ſpirit of profecie that was in hym, aftir the witneſſyng of Jheſu Criſt, the Sone of God, that ſchulde come to ayenbie his puple fro her ſynnes, and the hooli ordre bi that he was clepid / And thus it is writun / Amen.

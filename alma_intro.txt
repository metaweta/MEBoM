The account of Alma, that was the ſone of Alma, the firſte and chief iuge ouer the puple of Nephi, and alſo the hiȝ preeſt ouer the chirche. An account of the regne of the iuges, and of the werris and ſtrifis among the puple. And alſo an account of a werre bitwixe the Nephitis and the Lamanytis, aftir the record of Alma, the firſte and chief iuge.

2 Nephi Chapter 11

¶ 11:1 And now, Jacob ſpak manye moo thingis to my puple in that tyme; netheles oonli theſe thingis Y haue maad to be writun, for the thingis whiche Y haue writun ſufficen to me.
¶ 11:2 ⁊ now Y, Nephi, write more of Yſaies wordis, for my ſoule delitith in hiſe wordis / For Y ſchal likne hiſe wordis to my puple, and Y ſchal ſende hem forth to alle my ſones, for he treuli ſiȝ my Aȝenbiere, euen as Y haue ſeyn hym.
¶ 11:3 ⁊ my brother Jacob alſo hath ſeyn hym as Y haue ſeyn hym; wherfor, Y ſchal ſende her wordis forth to my ſones to preue to hem that my wordis ben trewe / Wherfor, bi the wordis of thre, God hath ſeid, Y ſchal ſtabliſche my word / Netheles God ſendith moo witneſſis, and he preueth alle hiſe wordis.
¶ 11:4 Lo! My ſoule delitith in preuynge to my puple the treuthe of Criſtis comynge; for, for this ende hath the lawe of Moiſes be ȝyue; and alle thingis whiche han be ȝyue of God fro the bigynnyng of the world, to man, ben the figurynge of hym.
¶ 11:5 ⁊ alſo my ſoule delitith in the couenauntis of the Lord whiche he hath maad to oure fadris; ya, my ſoule delitith in his grace, and in his iuſtice, and power, and mercy in the greet and euerlaſtynge purpos of delyueraunce fro deth.
¶ 11:6 ⁊ my ſoule delitith in preuynge to my puple that but if Criſt ſchulde come, alle men moten nedis periſche.
¶ 11:7 For if ther be no Criſt, ther be no God; and if ther be no God, we ben not, for ther myȝte haue be no creacioun / But ther is a God, and he is Criſt, and he cometh in the fulneſſe of his owne tyme.
¶ 11:8 ⁊ now Y write ſumme of Yſaies wordis, that who ſo of my puple ſchal ſe theſe wordis moun lifte vp her hertis and be glad for alle men / Now theſe ben the wordis, and ye moun likne hem to you and to alle men.

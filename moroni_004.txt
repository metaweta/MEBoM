Moroni Chapter 4

¶ 4:1 The maner of her elderis and preeſtis mynyſtringe the fleiſch and blood of Criſt to the chirche; and thei mynyſtriden it bi the comaundementis of Criſt; wherfor we witen that the maner is trewe; and the eldere or preeſt it mynyſtride,
¶ 4:2 ⁊ thei knelden doun with the chirche, and preieden to the fadir in the name of Criſt, and ſeiden,
¶ 4:3 O God, the euerlaſtynge fadir, we axen thee, in the name of thi ſone Jheſu Criſt, to bleſſe and to halewe this breed to the ſoulis of alle tho that taken of it; that thei mowe ete it in remembraunce of thi ſones bodi, and witneſſen to thee, O God, the euerlaſtynge fadir, that thei ben willynge to take on hem the name of thi ſone, and euer to remembre hym, and to kepe hiſe comaundementis whiche he hath ȝouun to hem, that thei mowe alwei haue his ſpirit to be with hem / Amen.

Moroni Chapter 3

¶ 3:1 The maner wherin the diſciplis, that weren clepide the elde men of the chirche, preeſtis and techeris ordeyneden,
¶ 3:2 Whanne thei hadden preied to the fadir in the name of Criſt, thei leiden her hondis on hem, and ſeiden,
¶ 3:3 In the name of Jheſu Criſt Y ordeyn thee to be a preeſt, (or if he be a techer, Y ordeyn thee to be a techer,) to preche penaunce and remyſſioun of ſynnes bi Jheſu Criſt, bi the enduryng of feith on his name til in to the ende / Amen.
¶ 3:4 ⁊ aftir this maner thei ordeyneden preeſtis and techeris, bi the ȝiftis and clepyngis of God to men; and thei ordeyneden hem bi the power of the Hooli Gooſt, that was in hem.

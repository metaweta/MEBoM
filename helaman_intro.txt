An account of the Nephitis. Her werris and ſtrifis, and her diſſenſiouns. And alſo the prophecies of manye holi profetis, bifore the comyng of Criſt, aftir the recordis of Helaman, that was the ſone of Helaman, and alſo aftir the recordis of hiſe ſones, ȝhe to the comyng of Criſt. And alſo manye of the Lamanytis ben conuertid. An account of her conuerſioun. An account of the riȝtwiſneſſe of the Lamanytis, and of the wickidneſſe and abhomynaciouns of the Nephitis, aftir the record of Helaman and of hiſe ſones, ȝhe to the comyng of Criſt, whiche is clepid the book of Helaman, and ſo forth.

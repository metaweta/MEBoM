2 Nephi Chapter 22

¶ 22:1 And in that dai thou ſchalt ſeie, Lord, Y ſchal herie thee; for thouy thou were wrooth to me, thi wrath is turned awei, and thou haſt comfortid me.
¶ 22:2 Lo! God is myn helthe; Y ſchal triſte, and ſchal not drede; for the Lord JEHOUAH is my ſtrengthe and my ſong, and he is maad myn helthe.
¶ 22:3 Therfor with ioye ye ſchulen drawe watris out of the wellis of ſaluacioun.
¶ 22:4 ⁊ in that dai ye ſchulen ſeie, Heriye ye the Lord, and clepe ye his name; ſchewe ye his werkis among the puple, and ſeie ye that his name is maad hooli.
¶ 22:5 Synge ye to the Lord, for he hath do worſchipfuli; telle ye this in al the erthe.
¶ 22:6 Crye out and ſchoute, thou dwellere of Sion, for greet is the Hooli Oon of Iſrael in the myddis of thee.

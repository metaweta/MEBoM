3 Nephi Chapter 25

¶ 25:1 For lo! The dai cometh that ſchal brenne as an ouene; and alle the proude, ȝhe, and alle that don wickidli, ſchulen be ſtobil; and the dai that cometh ſchal brenne hem vp, ſeith the Lord of ooſtis, that it ſchal leue to hem nether roote ne braunche.
¶ 25:2 But to you that dreden my name, ſchal the Sone of Riȝtfulneſſe ariſe with heelyng in hiſe wyngis; and ye ſchulen go forth and wexe as calues in the ſtalle.
¶ 25:3 ⁊ ye ſchulen trede doun the wickid; for thei ſchulen be aiſchis vndur the ſoolis of youre feet in the dai that Y ſchal do this thing, ſeith the Lord of ooſtis.
¶ 25:4 Haue ye mynde of the lawe of Moiſes, my ſeruaunt, which Y comaundide to hym in Oreb for al Iſrael, with the ſtatutis and domes.
¶ 25:5 Lo! Y ſchal ſende to you Elie, the prophete, bifor the comynge of the greet and dredful dai of the Lord;
¶ 25:6 ⁊ he ſchal turne the herte of fadris to the ſones, and the herte of ſones to her fadris, leſt Y come, and ſmyte the erthe with curs.

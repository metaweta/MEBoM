An account of Lethi and of Saraie hiſe wijf, and of hiſe foure ſones, that ben clepid (bigynnyng at the eldeſt) Laman, Lemuel, Sam, and Nephi. The Lord warnith Lethi to departe out of the lond of Jeruſalem, for he prophecieth to the puple of her wickidneſſe, and thei ſeken to diſtrie hiſe lijf. He takith a iourney of thre daies in to the deſert with hiſe meynee. Nephi takith hiſe britheren and turneth aȝen to the lond of Jeruſalem aftir the record of the Jewis. The account of her ſuffryngis. Thei taken the douȝtris of Iſmael to wijf. Thei taken her meynees and departen in to the deſert. Her ſuffryngis and turmentis in the deſert. The cours of her iourneiyng. Thei comen to the grete watris. Nephies britheren rebellen aȝens hym. He confoundith hem, and bildith a ſchip. Thei clepen the name of the place Bountiful. Thei paſſen ouer the grete watris in to the biheeſt lond, and ſo forth. This is aftir the account of Nephi; ether in othere wordis, Y, Nephi, wroot this record.

Ether Chapter 5

¶ 5:1 And now Y, Moroni, haue writun the wordis whiche weren comaundid me, aftir my mynde; and Y haue told you the thingis whiche Y haue ſeelid vp; therfor touche hem not that ye moun tranſlate; for that thing is forbedun you, ſaue bi and bi it ſchal be wiſdom in God.
¶ 5:2 ⁊ lo! Ye moun be preuylegid that ye moun ſchewe the platis to hem that ſchulen helpe to bringe forth this werk;
¶ 5:3 ⁊ to thre ſchulen thei be ſchewid bi the power of God; wherfor thei ſchulen wite of a ſureti that theſe thingis ben trewe.
¶ 5:4 ⁊ in the mouth of thre witneſſis ſchulen theſe thingis be ſtabliſchid; and the witneſſyng of thre, and this werk, wherin ſchal be ſchewid the power of God and alſo his word, of whiche the Fadir, and the Sone, and the Hooli Gooſt beren record; and al this ſchal ſtonde as a witneſſyng aȝens the world in the laſte dai.
¶ 5:5 ⁊ if it ſo be that thei doon penaunce and comen to the Fadir in the name of Jheſu, thei ſchulen be reſſeyued in to the kyngdom of God.
¶ 5:6 ⁊ now, if Y haue not auctorite for theſe thingis, deme ye; for ye ſchulen knowe that Y haue auctorite whanne ye ſchulen ſe me, and we ſchulen ſtonde bifor God in the laſte dai / Amen.

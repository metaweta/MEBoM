Moroni Chapter 6

¶ 6:1 And now Y ſpeke of baptym / Lo! Elde men, preeſtis, and techeris weren baptiſid; and thei weren not baptiſid but if thei brouȝten forth fruit mete that thei weren worthi of it.
¶ 6:2 Nether reſſeyueden thei ony to baptym, but if thei camen forth with a broken herte and a contrit ſpirit, and witneſſiden to the chirche that thei treuli hadden do penaunce of alle her ſynnes.
¶ 6:3 ⁊ noon was reſſeyued to baptym, but if thei token on hem the name of Criſt, hauynge a determynacioun to ſerue hym til to the ende.
¶ 6:4 ⁊ whanne thei hadden be reſſeyued to baptym, and weren wrouȝt on and clenſid bi the power of the Hooli Gooſt, thei weren noumbrid among the puple of Criſtis chirche; and her names weren takun, that thei myȝten be remembrid and nurſchid bi the good word of God, to kepe hem in the riȝt weie, to kepe hem contynuelli wakynge to preier, triſtynge oonli on the meritis of Criſt, that was the auctor and the endere of her feith.
¶ 6:5 ⁊ the chirche cam togidere ofte, to faſte and to preie, and to ſpeke oon with anothir of the wele of her ſoulis.
¶ 6:6 ⁊ thei camen togidere ofte to take breed and wyn, in remembraunce of the Lord Jheſu.
¶ 6:7 ⁊ thei weren ſtreit to kepe that ther ſchulde be no wickidneſſe among hem; and who ſo was foundun to do wickidneſſe, and thre witneſſis of the chirche hym condempneden bifor the elderis, and if he dide not penaunce, and knowlechide not, his name was doon awei, and he was not noumbrid among the puple of Criſt.
¶ 6:8 But as ofte as thei diden penaunce and ſouȝten foryyueneſſe with verry entent, thei weren foryouun.
¶ 6:9 ⁊ her metyngis weren gouerned of the chirche aftir the maner of the worchyngis of the ſpirit, and bi the power of the Hooli Gooſt; for as the power of the Hooli Gooſt ledde hem, wher to preche, or to moneſte, or to preie, or to biſeche, or to ſynge, ſo it was doon.

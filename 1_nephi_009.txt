1 Nephi Chapter 9

¶ 9:1 Alle theſe thingis my fadir ſiȝ, and herde, and ſpak, as he dwellide in a tente in the vale of Lemuel; and alſo ful manye mo thingis, whiche moun not be writun on theſe tables.
¶ 9:2 ⁊ now, as Y haue ſpoken of theſe plates, lo! Thei ben not the plates on whiche Y make a ful acount of my puplis ſtorie; for the plates on whiche Y make a ful acount of my puple, Y haue youe the name of Nephi; wherfor thei ben clepid Nephies plates, aftir myn owne name; and theſe plates alſo ben clepid Nephies plates.
¶ 9:3 Netheles, Y haue reſceyued a comaundement of the Lord, that Y theſe plates ſchulde make, for the ſpecial purpos that of my puplis mynyſterie an acount ſchulde be grauun.
¶ 9:4 On the othere plates ſchulde be grauun an acount of the kyngis regne, and of my puplis batels and ſtryues; wherfor theſe plates ben for the more part of the mynyſterie, and the othere plates ben for the more part of the kyngis regne and of my puplis batels and ſtryues.
¶ 9:5 Wherfor, the Lord hath comaundid me theſe plates to make, for a wiſe purpos in hym, which purpos Y woot not.
¶ 9:6 But the Lord knowith alle thingis fro the bigynnyng; wherfor he makith redi a weie to fulfille alle hiſe werkis among the ſones of men; for lo! He hath alle power to the fulfillyng of alle hiſe wordis / And ſo it is / Amen.

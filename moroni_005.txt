Moroni Chapter 5

¶ 5:1 The maner of mynyſtringe the wyn / Lo! Thei token the cuppe, and ſeiden,
¶ 5:2 O God, the euerlaſtynge fadir, we axen thee, in the name of thi ſone Jheſu Criſt, to bleſſe and to halewe this wyn to the ſoulis of alle tho that drynken of it, that thei do it in remembraunce of thi ſones blood, that was ſched for hem; that thei witneſſen to thee, O God, the euerlaſtynge fadir, that thei do alwei remembre hym, that thei mowe haue his ſpirit to be with hem / Amen.

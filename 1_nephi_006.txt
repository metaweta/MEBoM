1 Nephi Chapter 6

¶ 6:1 And now Y, Nephi, ȝyue not the linage of my fadris in this part of my book, nether at ony tyme aftir ſchal Y ȝyue it on theſe tables that Y am writynge; for it is ȝouun in the book that my fadir hath kept; wherfor Y write it not in this werk.
¶ 6:2 For it ſuffiſith me to ſeie that we ben of the ſeed of Joſeph.
¶ 6:3 ⁊ it mateereth not to me that Y be biſy to ȝyue a ful acount of alle my fadris thingis, for thei mowen not be writun on theſe tables, for Y deſire the roome that Y may write of the thingis of God.
¶ 6:4 For the fulneſſe of myn entent is that Y may counſeile men to come to the God of Abraham, and the God of Yſaac, and the God of Jacob, and be maad ſaaf.
¶ 6:5 Wherfor the thingis that ben pleſynge to the world Y write not, but the thingis that ben pleſynge to God and to hem that ben not of the world.
¶ 6:6 Wherfor Y ſchal ȝyue comaundement to my ſeed, that thei ſchulen not ocupie theſe tables with thingis that ben not of worth to the ſones of men.
